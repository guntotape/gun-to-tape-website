00:45 The Philosophy

Two words. One idea.

In athletics, gun to tape is the whole race — the full distance, timed and true, start to finish. We borrowed the name because it's also what we make: tape. Real footage, properly shot and properly cut, that still means something years from now.

Built to last

Long-form masters, re-cut for any channel, any year, any anniversary that comes after.

Shot like a film

Multi-camera coverage, proper sound, proper light — and a crew who understand the sport.

Story before highlights

The training, the setbacks, the start-line nerves — not just the medal on the line.

02:10 What We Make

Content that earns its shelf life.

Six formats, one standard — every film built to be watched again long after it's published.

Athlete Profile Films

Character-led documentary shorts and features that tell an athlete's story beyond the results page.

Race Day Coverage

Multi-camera cinematic coverage from the call room to the finish tape, cut for broadcast and archive alike.

Sponsor & Partner Films

Training & Process Series

Championship Documentaries

Feature-length films for major meets, built to be watched again for years — not just one season.

04:30 How We Work

From brief to tape.

Four stages, every time — the same discipline whether it's one race or a whole season.

  1. 00:00

    Brief

    We start with the story: the athletes, the season, the reason the film needs to exist.

  2. 00:20

    Shoot

    On location for as long as the story needs — race day, training ground, home track, dawn if that's when it happens.

  3. 00:35

    Cut

    Editing, colour and sound, shaped into a film that holds up on the big screen or the small one.

  4. 00:50

    Deliver

    Long-form masters plus every cut you need — built to be pulled out and reused for years, not one campaign.
